<h2>Collecting and Using Your Personal Information</h2>
<h3>Types of Data Collected</h3>

<h4>Personal Data</h4>
<p>While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information to enable core application features.</p>

<h4>Health Data & Health Connect</h4>
<p>If You grant explicit permission within the Application, Our Service accesses, collects, and writes health and fitness data through Android Health Connect. Specific Health Data accessed and processed includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Exercise & Workout Data:</strong> Information regarding Your completed workout sessions, exercise names, sets, reps, duration, and exercise timestamps (accessed via <code>android.permission.health.READ_EXERCISE</code> and written via <code>android.permission.health.WRITE_EXERCISE</code>).</li>
<li><strong>Body Weight Data:</strong> Information regarding Your recorded body weight entries and timestamps (accessed via <code>android.permission.health.READ_WEIGHT</code> and written via <code>android.permission.health.WRITE_WEIGHT</code>).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>On-Device Processing:</strong> All Health Data accessed through Health Connect is processed locally on Your Device to display Your workout logs, calculate training volume and active streaks, generate bodyweight progress graphs, and provide progressive overload coaching recommendations.</p>

<h4>Usage Data</h4>
<p>Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service, including mobile device unique IDs, operating system type, and diagnostic performance data.</p>

<h3>Use of Your Personal Data</h3>
<p>The Company uses Personal Data and Health Data strictly for the following purposes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>To provide and maintain Our Service:</strong> Displaying Your workout history, training volume, personal records, and body weight trends.</li>
<li><strong>To manage Your requests:</strong> Attending to Your data export, backup, or restoration requests.</li>
<li><strong>Health Connect Synchronization:</strong> Syncing Your workout logs and body weight records with Android Health Connect upon Your explicit request and consent.</li>
</ul>
