### 🧠 [kask — Private On-Device AI](https://github.com/rhuta/kask)

**kask** is a private artificial intelligence assistant designed to run entirely on your device.

**No cloud. No data mining. No subscription required for core features.**

Whether you're summarizing sensitive documents, analyzing private photos, or transcribing meetings, your data stays on your phone.

## 🔒 Privacy by Design

kask is built around a simple principle:

> **Your private data should stay private.**

AI processing happens directly on your device rather than requiring your content to be uploaded to a remote server.

### 📝 Smart Text Processing

* **Summarize** — Turn long articles and PDFs into concise bullet points.
* **Rewrite & Tone** — Polish emails or instantly change the tone of your writing.
* **Grammar Fix** — Correct spelling and grammar with professional-quality assistance.
* **Extract** — Pull key facts, information, and data points from any text.

### 🖼️ Vision & Image Analysis

* **Describe** — Understand what's contained in your photos.
* **OCR** — Extract text from images, menus, screenshots, and documents directly into Markdown.
* **Ask About Images** — Interact with your visuals to find specific information.

### 🎤 Secure Transcription

* **Voice to Text** — Convert audio recordings into text transcripts.
* **Meeting Notes** — Turn recorded conversations into concise, actionable insights.

### 🚀 Pro-Grade Performance

kask is designed to scale its AI capabilities according to your device.

* **Efficient — 0.8B**
  Optimized for speed and lower resource usage.

* **Balanced — 2B**
  A balance between performance and reasoning capability.

* **Precision — 4B**
  Designed for more complex tasks requiring additional reasoning capacity.

* **📱 Smart Hardware Detection**
  kask automatically adapts to your device's available RAM and CPU capabilities.

* **📝 Markdown Support**
  Responses are beautifully formatted and ready to export to your favorite notes application.

> **Powerful AI. Private by default. Running where your data is.**
